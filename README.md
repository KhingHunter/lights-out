# LIGHTS OUT

It's 1:12 AM in October 2006. You have school in five hours. One more run.

You are a kid in bed, flat on your back, holding a silver DS Lite. On it you're playing **NO COMPLY**, a 3D skate game where tricks are flicked with the stylus. When the hall light clicks on under the door, you have about two seconds to slam the lid, shove the DS under your pillow, close your eyes, and fake sleep. Get caught and it's confiscated, and the night starts over.

Seven nights, Monday to Sunday, each with its own NO COMPLY level and a new problem in the house.

## Download

**[LIGHTS OUT for Mac](https://github.com/KhingHunter/lights-out/releases/latest)** (macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon and Intel). Free.

1. Download `LightsOut.dmg` from the latest release and open it.
2. Drag Lights Out to Applications.
3. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't check the app (it is signed but not notarized yet). Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**. After that it opens normally.

Keyboard and mouse, or a gamepad.
